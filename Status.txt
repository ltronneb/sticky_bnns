    Sparsity is the share of weights (no biases or BatchNorm) exactly zero,
    averaged over draws.
  ☐ Error bars. Covered in the text (SEM over 5 splits for UCI, R-hat for
    convergence). Say once that toys and images are single runs without error bars.
  ☐ Compute paragraph (draft)
    Experiments ran on an Apple M3 laptop (8 cores, 16 GB), used for the toy and
    small UCI runs, and on a server with two Intel Xeon Gold 6226R CPUs (64 threads,
    753 GB RAM) and four NVIDIA A10 GPUs (24 GB each). The medium UCI runs and NUTS
    used one CPU core per run, and the image models one GPU each. A toy PDMP run took
    about 6 minutes, a small UCI run about 30 minutes, a medium UCI run 2.5 to 4.5
    hours, the MNIST models about 16 hours and ResNet-20 about 2 days.

11d, small at 1e7 (uci_convergence.ipynb, chains 1 to 4, NUTS from MAPs 1 to 4)
  Every sampler converged. R-hat at most 1.01 for log sigma and test RMSE, except
  sticky Boomerang w 0.1 (1.04 on log sigma, ESS 1.6 per 1e6 grads). Between-chain SD
  of the test RMSE at most 0.01. So on the small network even the Boomerang family
  forgets its MAP, unlike on the medium network.

16, LBBNN tuned (notes/lbbnn_tuning, 2 splits). Remaining gap to the samplers on energy
  and yacht is the method (coarse fit under uncertain inclusions, learned sigma 2 to 4x
  the samplers'), not the noise prior. The learned model prior was unstable (sparsity
  21 to 99 %) and is not used.

11c, medium at 1e7 (uci_convergence.ipynb)
  ZigZag family converged, R-hat 1.01 to 1.04 for log sigma and test RMSE, every
  chain reaches the same values whatever its MAP. Boomerang family stays at its
  starting MAP, R-hat 1.6 to 2.0, chain means follow the MAP's test RMSE. Boomerang
  chains keep a lower log sigma than ZigZag and NUTS (-1.54 to -1.74 against -1.40).
  Explanation (supported, not tested), an overfit MAP has a low sigma, the Fisher
  precision scales roughly with 1/sigma^2, so the reference is narrow and the orbits
  small. MAPs 1 to 3 (lowest log sigma) have 2 to 3 times the weight precision of MAP 0.
  Between-chain SD of test RMSE 0.01 to 0.09 except medium Boomerang (0.40 to 0.49),
  against a between-split SD of about 0.45.

14, large, full batch (deep_wide_convergence.ipynb, ZigZag and NUTS so far)
  ZigZag R-hat 1.03 log sigma, 1.05 train RMSE, 1.01 test RMSE. NUTS 1.00.

11, convergence chains at 1e6, small and medium, boston split 0
  small   ZigZag and sticky ZigZag converged, R-hat at most 1.03. Boomerang family slow
          but not stuck, chain means agree, R-hat up to 1.1 to 1.2 on train RMSE.
  medium  ZigZag family partly mixed, R-hat 1.1 to 1.5. Boomerang family stays at its
          starting MAP, R-hat 1.6 to 2.3.
  NUTS (11b, 4 MAPs x 4 chains of 1000) converged on both networks, R-hat at most 1.006.

10, skeletons (uci_skeletons.ipynb)
  0 violations in all 12 runs. Grads per event 2.1 to 3.7 (sticky), 4.1 ZigZag, 6.6
  Boomerang. Freezes and thaws are 25 to 50 % of sticky ZigZag events and 86 to 97 %
  of sticky Boomerang events. Path ESS is low (single digits to about 1000 of 4000).
  Grid (v1) vs single segment (v2), small network, w = 0.3, gradients per event v1 to
  v2, ZigZag 7.67 to 4.16, sticky ZigZag 8.58 to 3.41, Boomerang 11.01 to 6.64, sticky
  Boomerang 3.81 to 2.40.

  LeNet  ZigZag 0 violations, Boomerang 79, test accuracy 0.985 and 0.986
