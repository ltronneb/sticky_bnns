13. Thought experiment for tomorrow, grid vs single segment under a constant rate
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
Derivation and simulation in notes/constant_rate_cost (README.md, sim.py). The
simulation matches the formulas, gain about (K + 2) / 3 per bounce and 3 / 2 per
freeze or thaw.
With a constant rate lambda the tangent bound is exact (up to the 1 % inflation),
so rejections are about 1 per 100 proposals and balanced adaptation lets t_max grow
until most windows hold an event. Expected gradients per event, single segment
  after a bounce or freeze or thaw the cache is reset, 2 evals to build the window
  + 1 eval at the proposed time, so about 3 per bounce and 2 per freeze or thaw.
Grid with K segments needs about K + 1 evals to build the window, so about K + 2
per event, and the predicted gain is about (K + 2) / 3.
Check against the data
  v1 diagnostics store rate_evals and n_segments per window (the sparsity ablation
  *_skeleton.pt files in results/paper), so the real K and cost per window are known.
  Observed small network gains are 1.6 to 2.5 x (RESULTS NOTES, section 7), so either
  K was small or the rate is far from constant. Compare prediction and observation
  per sampler, and check the 3 and 2 floors against the v2 counts (bounce vs freeze
  and thaw shares) in the skeleton notebook.
